## The Los Alamos Neutron Science Center

<img class="banner" src="images/LANSCE-GIS.png" alt="The LANSCE site, looking along the linear accelerator" />

- 800 MeV proton linear accelerator
- Two pulsed spallation sources: The Lujan Center and the Weapons Neutron Research facility
- Access to 10 orders of magnitude in neutron energy (from sub-meV cold to ultra-MeV fast)
